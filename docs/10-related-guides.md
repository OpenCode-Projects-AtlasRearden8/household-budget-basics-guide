# Related Guides (The Companions)

This guide is one tool in a small family of plain-English household money tools, made to complement each other without repeating each other. Each guide does one job; together they cover the landscape.

## Envelope Budget Webapp

**Its job:** the *tool*. Digital envelopes for your categories — set money aside by category, spend from the envelope, watch balances.

**How it pairs with this guide:** This guide explains *why* categories matter and how to build your first plan on paper ([Chapter 1](01-paper-first-month.html)). When your categories feel settled, move them into the webapp and it takes over the day-to-day mechanics. Sinking funds from [Chapter 3](03-sinking-funds.html) translate directly into long-horizon envelopes.

## Subscription Audit Handbook

**Its job:** the *deep dive* on finding every recurring charge, deciding what earns its keep, and canceling with real-world scripts (including what to say when they offer a discount to stay).

**How it pairs with this guide:** Our [Yearly Sweep](06-yearly-sweep.html) chapter gives you the *when and what* — the once-a-year checklist. The handbook gives you the *how*, line by line. Run the sweep with both pages open.

## Credit & Debt Calm Guide

**Its job:** the *calm, complete* guide to credit scores, debt payoff methods, collections, and what to do when payments are genuinely behind.

**How it pairs with this guide:** This guide builds the stable floor (calendar, funds, smoothing) that makes debt work *possible*. When you're ready to work on debt specifically — payoff order, negotiating, protecting your credit — that guide has your back, with the same no-shame voice.

## Suggested order for a messy-start situation

1. **This guide, chapters 1–3, this week.** Paper plan, bills calendar, first three sinking funds. That's the floor.
2. **Subscription Audit Handbook** as your first yearly sweep ([Chapter 6](06-yearly-sweep.html)) — it usually finds the money that funds the rest.
3. **Envelope Budget Webapp** once categories are calm — it's the tool that makes the system easy to run forever.
4. **Credit & Debt Calm Guide** whenever debt or credit becomes the main event.

No step requires the others. Each guide stands alone; together they're a system.
