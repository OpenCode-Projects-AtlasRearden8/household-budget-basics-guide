# Start Here

Welcome. This is a budget guide for real households — the ones with variable hours, surprise expenses, kids, pets, medical bills, and a genuine soft spot for pizza night.

It is not a lecture. There is no diet, no "just stop buying coffee" talk, and no spreadsheet that judges you. Money math is emotional math, and every tool in here is designed to be doable on a tired Tuesday, not just a motivated Saturday morning.

## Who this is for

- Anyone who has ever looked at their balance and thought "wait, what happened?"
- Households whose income changes month to month (nurses, shift workers, gig workers, commission folks, seasonal work).
- People who were never taught this stuff and would like to learn it without feeling small.
- Couples and roommates who want to be on the same page without a fight.

## The one-paragraph version

A budget is not a punishment. It is a plan you write down **before** the month starts, so the money has jobs and the jobs are ones you chose. This guide walks you through one "paper-first" practice month, a bills calendar so due dates stop ambushing you, sinking funds so big bills stop being *emergencies*, a paycheck-smoothing system for irregular income, a splurge line on purpose, a once-a-year sweep of insurance and memberships, and a weekly ten-minute money date to keep it all alive.

## How to use this book

1. Read [The Paper-First Month](01-paper-first-month.html) and try the practice month: spending on paper before you spend in real life.
2. Set up the [Bills Calendar](02-bills-calendar.html) and [Sinking Funds](03-sinking-funds.html). These two alone end most "ugh, money" moments.
3. If your paychecks are different every time, [Irregular Paycheck Smoothing](04-irregular-paychecks.html) is your chapter.
4. Skim the rest as needed. The [Worksheets](08-worksheets.html) and [Glossary](09-glossary.html) live at the back like an appendix in a cookbook.

## What this guide does *not* do

On purpose. These topics have their own companion guides — different jobs, different tools:

- **Envelope (cash-style) budgeting mechanics and apps** → *Envelope Budget Webapp*. This guide explains *why* you'd want categories; that project is the tool itself.
- **Canceling and auditing subscriptions line by line** → *Subscription Audit Handbook*. Our [Yearly Sweep](06-yearly-sweep.html) tells you when to do the sweep; that handbook does the audit in detail.
- **Debt payoff methods, credit scores, and collections** → *Credit & Debt Calm Guide*. We'll point you there and stay out of its lane.

## Plain-language promise

Every term and sentence in this guide is intended to be understandable at the end of a long shift, in the glow of a kitchen light, with no financial background. If something reads as jargon, the [Glossary](09-glossary.html) will untangle it.

You've got this. Genuinely.
