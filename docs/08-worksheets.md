# Worksheets

All worksheets are fill-in pages: type into them in your browser (they save locally on your device — nothing is uploaded anywhere, ever) and print for the fridge. They're plain pages on purpose: if a worksheet needs an internet account to work, it violates the whole spirit of this guide.

Every worksheet opens in a new tab from this list, or from links in the chapters where they're first mentioned.

## How to use them well

- **Fill in, don't fret.** Pencil numbers you'll revisit. The paper-first month is a rehearsal — wrong guesses are the *point*; they get corrected next month.
- **One shared copy per household.** Duplicate the page (print twice, or copy the browser-link) if two people want to fill at the same time. Just keep the *live* copy shared.
- **Print the bills calendar.** It earns its permanent spot on the fridge or inside a cabinet door more than any other page here.
- **Local first, literally.** Your typed numbers live in your browser's local storage on your device. To start fresh on a worksheet, use its "Clear" button.

## What's *not* in the worksheets, on purpose

- No account linking, no bank sync, no email capture, no "premium version."
- No required math beyond round numbers and dividing by 12. The [Variable Income worksheet](../worksheets/variable-income.html) will do the median for you, because median math is the one thing nobody enjoys.

If a worksheet on paper works better for you than typing, print and go — the guide works identically either way.
