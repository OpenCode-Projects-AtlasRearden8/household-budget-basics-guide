# Glossary

Plain-English definitions, tied to where each idea lives in this guide. No shame, no jargon chains.

## A

**Annual sweep** — The once-a-year hour where you re-check insurance, memberships, and subscriptions. [Chapter 6](06-yearly-sweep.html).

**Auto-pay** — A bill that drains your account automatically on its due date. Great for fixed bills; keep variable bills (electric) visible on the [bills calendar](02-bills-calendar.html) so surprises still get seen.

## B

**Baseline (income)** — Your chosen steady monthly income number, from the middle (median) of your last several months. The number your plan runs on. [Chapter 4](04-irregular-paychecks.html).

**Bills calendar** — One page listing every recurring bill by due day, all twelve months. [Chapter 2](02-bills-calendar.html).

**Buffer (line)** — A small plan category ($25–50 to start) that absorbs real life: a copay, a price jump, a forgotten thing. Not a punishment jar; a landing pad. [Chapter 1](01-paper-first-month.html).

## C

**Category** — A labeled job for money (Groceries, Gas, Pets, Splurge). Categories can be lines on paper, envelopes of cash, or buckets in a bank app — the label does the work.

**Category refresh** — The once-a-month update of your paper plan's numbers, based on what the weekly dates actually taught you.

## D

**Due date** — The day a bill says "money, now." The bills calendar collects all of them in one view so they stop ambushing you.

## E

**Envelope budgeting** — A method where each category's money is set aside separately (historically in cash envelopes) and you spend only from its envelope. The companion project **Envelope Budget Webapp** does this digitally.

## F

**Fund refill** — Putting money back into a sinking fund after it's used, per Plan 2 in [Chapter 3](03-sinking-funds.html).

## H

**Holding Account** — A separate savings account where all paychecks land first, so checking behaves like a steady paycheck even when income isn't. The core tool of [Chapter 4](04-irregular-paychecks.html). (Also called a smoothing account or income-smoothing buffer.)

## I

**Irregular income** — Income that changes in amount or timing paycheck to paycheck: shift differentials, overtime, tips, gig work, commissions, seasonal work. Handled in [Chapter 4](04-irregular-paychecks.html), never by pretending it's fixed.

## L

**Lumpy bill** — Any bill that arrives rarely and large: registration, insurance premium, holidays, camp tuition, vet bills. Lumpy bills are why [sinking funds](03-sinking-funds.html) exist.

## M

**Money date** — The weekly ten-minute check-in. Five small steps, one small win, timer on. [Chapter 7](07-money-date.html).

## N

**No-shame rule** — The guide-wide policy that a blown category is information, not moral failing. Enforced in [Chapter 5](05-splurge-line.html) and everywhere else.

## P

**Paper-first month** — The practice month where you give next month's income a plan *on paper* before it arrives. [Chapter 1](01-paper-first-month.html).

**Paycheck smoothing** — Making your *plan* steady even when your *pay* waves around, using a Baseline and a Holding Account. [Chapter 4](04-irregular-paychecks.html).

## R

**Renewal trap** — A service that got cheaper to join than to leave (annual "deals," auto-renewals). Caught by the yearly sweep; untangled by the **Subscription Audit Handbook**.

## S

**Sinking fund** — Small monthly set-asides for known-but-lumpy expenses, each with a name. Car, health/pets, holidays are the starting three. [Chapter 3](03-sinking-funds.html).

**Splurge line** — A real, planned line for joy in your budget, protected from guilt by policy. [Chapter 5](05-splurge-line.html).

**Starter reserve** — What your buffer grows into when unused: a few hundred dollars of "the plan absorbed it." Precursor to any emergency fund advice you'll find elsewhere.

## V

**Variable income** — See Irregular income.

## W

**Weekly money date** — See Money date.

**Worksheet** — A fill-in page from [Chapter 8](08-worksheets.html), printable or type-in-the-browser.
