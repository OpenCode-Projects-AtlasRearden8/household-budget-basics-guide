Weekly Ten-Minute Money Date

Week of · timer on, five small steps. See Chapter 7.

  1. Check the engine light (1 min)
    Checking looks roughly normal?
    Holding Account okay?
  2. Look ahead 7–10 days (3 min)
    Bill due before next dateAmountFunded?
  3. Skim the last 7 days (2–4 min — tallies only, no judgment)
    Outside-the-plan spend (category + note)
    Category it came from (or buffer)
  4. Fund the funds (1–2 min)
    Fund transfers sent (names)
    Any fund loans to log + refill plan
  5. One small win (1 min)
    This week's small win
    Next week's one focus

Roughly-normal weeks need only steps 1, 2 and 5. The short version is still the full system.
